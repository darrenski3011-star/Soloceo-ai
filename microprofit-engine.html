Before real-world use

  • Obtain data through authorized APIs and comply with provider terms.
  • Confirm executable quotes, timing, liquidity, settlement rules, taxes, and every fee.
  • Investigate ledger discrepancies with the account or payment provider; never treat them as ownerless money.
  • Keep any future trading or money movement disabled until independent testing, legal review, explicit approval, hard limits, audit logs, and an emergency stop are in place.

This tool is for research and accounting analysis, not financial, legal, or investment advice. Simulated estimates are not a promise of profit.
